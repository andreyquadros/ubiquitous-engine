# ubiqX AI — launch film, draft B: "Você não deu play."

> **Angle B — relentless product montage** (Linear / Raycast). Human-readable twin of `draft-b.json` (generated from the same data; the JSON is the source of truth).
>
> 1920×1080 · 30 fps · 120 BPM (beat 15 f, bar 60 f) · **1440 frames = 48.0 s = 24 bars** · 19 scenes · on-screen language PT-BR · no voice-over, built to work muted.

## The idea

The film never explains the product; it **uses** it. Frame 0 is already the real dashboard, tilted hard in 3D under one line — **"Você não deu play."** — and inside the first second the window slams flat and the camera dives in. On X the video autoplays without the viewer pressing play either, so the hook works twice. From there every beat is one idea proven on the real UI: the day that recorded itself, the category you describe, the chain that decides (rules, memory, AI), a queue that only asks about doubts, the key **1**, **"Confirmar os 19"**, UBI jumping out of the empty queue, the report that writes itself, the focus guard closing YouTube, the AI you choose. A two-bar breather on privacy, a four-verb crescendo over a wall of screens, **one beat of silence**, and the wordmark drops.

**Hook (one line):** the real ubiqX dashboard, tilted 26° under "Você não deu play.", slams flat and the camera dives into "88 de foco" before frame 30.

**Three strongest moments**
1. **The key "1" (S05→S07, 0:09.5–0:15.0):** band stop, a physical keycap pressed alone in the silence on the beat, then the keycap *flies into* the UI and becomes the "1 · IFRO" chip, and the doubtful group slides out of the queue as the groove crashes back in.
2. **"Confirmar os 19" → UBI jumps out of the UI (S08→S09, 0:15.0–0:20.5):** a cursor click on the bar-9 downbeat turns the whole list mint ("você" 100 %), then the empty queue's in-app UBI is match-cut to the 3D UBI, who jumps and lands on the backbeat: "Nada em dúvida!".
3. **Crescendo → silence → drop (S16→S18, 0:36.0–0:42.0):** "Captura. Classifica. Revisa. Entrega." slam on four beats over a 3D wall of real screens, the film's own hero moments flash on 8ths and 16ths, the volt X appears alone, 15 frames of total silence, and "ubiqX AI" drops with the sub boom.

## Conventions

- **grid:** beat = 15 f, bar = 60 f; every scene start is a multiple of 15
- **relativeFrames:** all frames inside a scene (copy, camera, cursor, motion, sfx) are relative to the scene startFrame
- **uiPlane:** Screen width W = 1600 px unless a scene says otherwise (window 1600 x 1000 at zoom 1). On-screen UI size = capture px x (W / 2880) x zoom; bitmap upsampling = (W / 2880) x zoom.
- **textBearingZoom:** any UI text we zoom INTO is >= 36 px on screen (see zoomCheck). Where that needs bitmap upsampling > 1.15, a crisp patch is mandatory (live HTML re-typeset of the exact UI string/element glued to the hotspot as a Screen child, same font/size/colour), and everything outside the hotspot gets DOF blur >= 4 px + dim >= 0.5. Preferred alternative for the patch: recapture that hotspot at DPR 4 with tools/capture-ui.mjs (newPage supports dpr).
- **copyFields:** inFrame = first frame of the entrance; landFrame = frame the last word/line has landed (spring >= 0.9); outFrame = first frame of the exit or the cut; hold = outFrame - landFrame >= max(24, 2 x chars) (slam words: >= 12 f each, final >= 30 f)
- **transitions:** no overlapping TransitionSeries: each transition is split into an outgoing half at the end of scene N (framesOut) and an incoming half at the start of scene N+1 (framesIn), same type name and total frames on both sides; symmetric transitions have their midpoint on the cut frame
- **sfx:** atFrame = frame where the HIT (hit_offset of sfx-manifest) must land, relative to the scene; build one master SFX track at absolute frames (scene start + atFrame - hit_offset) so tails and lead-ins can cross scene boundaries. gainDb is relative to the bed nominal level (style 7.3).
- **camera:** camera[].atFrame = arrival frame; the move runs over the frames given in ease; zoom interpolates in log space; tilt = plane rotateX/rotateY (deg); anchor = screen point where the target lands (Screen CameraKey.anchor)
- **cursor:** cursor[].atFrame = arrival (click frames are on beats); rendered in screen space, 30-34 px tall, scale capped at 1.5x with camera zoom; enters from off-frame or fades in at a rest point, never teleports
- **privacy:** never used: timeline.png, timeline-selected.png, timeline-reclassify.png, reports-generated.png, og.png, any brand/ai/* provider logo. report-1-meta (model id) and settings model-fields are kept out of frame; no Gmail/e-mail row is ever a camera target.
